Instructions:
1. Prep Everything: Have all ingredients chopped, measured, and ready by the stove. Fried rice cooks very quickly!

2. Scramble the Eggs: Heat 1 tablespoon of cooking oil in your wok/skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Pour in the beaten eggs. Let them set slightly, then scramble gently until just cooked through (soft curds). Remove immediately to a clean plate.

3. Cook Aromatics & Vegetables: Add another tablespoon of oil to the wok, increase heat to high. Add the white/light green parts of the green onions, minced garlic, and ginger (if using). Stir-fry for 15-30 seconds until fragrant. 

Add the mixed vegetables (peas, carrots, onions, peppers, etc.). Stir-fry for 2-3 minutes until crisp-tender. If using raw ground pork, add it here and cook until browned and crumbled (drain excess fat if needed).

4. Cook the Pork: Push the vegetables to one side of the wok. Add the remaining tablespoon of oil to the empty space. Add the cooked, diced pork. Stir-fry for 1-2 minutes to heat through and get slightly crispy edges. Mix the pork with the vegetables.

5. Add the Rice: Add the cold, day-old rice to the wok. Break up any large clumps with your spatula. Stir-fry constantly for 2-3 minutes, pressing the rice against the hot surface of the wok to dry it out and get some nice toasted bits ("wok hei" at home!).

6.Combine & Season: Return the scrambled eggs to the wok, breaking them up into smaller pieces. Pour the soy sauce and oyster sauce evenly over the rice mixture. Add the white/black pepper and optional sugar/wine.

7. Stir-fry vigorously for another 1-2 minutes, ensuring everything is well combined and heated through. Taste and adjust seasoning (salt, pepper, soy sauce).

Finish:

Remove the wok from the heat. Drizzle the sesame oil over the top and add the dark green parts of the green onions. Toss everything together one final time.

Serve Immediately: Enjoy hot!
