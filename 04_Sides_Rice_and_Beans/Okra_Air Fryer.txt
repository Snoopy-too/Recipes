Air Fryer Crispy Okra
Active Time: 5 mins | Cook Time: 10–12 mins | Serves: 2–4

Ingredients:
1 lb (450g) fresh okra, stems trimmed (slice in half lengthwise if large)

1 tbsp olive oil or ghee (or spray oil)

½ tsp garlic powder

½ tsp smoked paprika (or cayenne for heat)

¼ tsp salt + black pepper

1 tsp lemon juice or vinegar (post-cooking)

Optional: 2 tbsp cornstarch or rice flour (for extra crunch)



Steps:
Prep Okra:

Rinse okra, then pat completely dry with paper towels (critical for crispiness).

Trim stems but keep pods whole (halve only if thicker than your thumb).

Season:

Toss okra with oil, spices, and salt in a bowl. For max crunch, add cornstarch and toss again.

Air Fry:

Preheat air fryer to 390°F (200°C) for 3 mins (if your model allows).

Arrange okra in a single layer (no overlapping!) in the basket. Work in batches if needed.

Cook for 10–12 mins, shaking the basket halfway, until browned and crispy.

Finish:

Spritz with lemon juice right after cooking.
