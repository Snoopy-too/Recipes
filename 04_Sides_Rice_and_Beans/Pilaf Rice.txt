Perfect Rice Pilaf Recipe

Ingredients
2 tablespoons butter
1 medium yellow onion, diced
3 garlic cloves, minced
1½ cups long-grain white rice (like basmati or jasmine)
3 cups chicken broth (or vegetable broth for a vegetarian option)
1 teaspoon salt
½ teaspoon ground black pepper
3 tablespoons chopped parsley (for garnish)

Instructions
Sauté Aromatics: In a large skillet, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the diced onion and minced garlic, cooking until the onion becomes translucent, about 5 minutes.
Toast the Rice: Add the rice to the skillet and stir constantly until it starts to turn lightly golden, about 4 minutes.
Add Broth: Pour in the chicken or vegetable broth, along with salt and pepper. Bring the mixture to a boil.
Simmer: Once boiling, cover the skillet with a lid and reduce the heat to low. Let it simmer for about 15 minutes without lifting the lid.
Rest: After 15 minutes, remove the skillet from heat and let it sit covered for an additional 5 minutes.
Fluff and Serve: Remove the lid, fluff the rice with a fork, and stir in the chopped parsley. Serve warm as a delightful side dish.
