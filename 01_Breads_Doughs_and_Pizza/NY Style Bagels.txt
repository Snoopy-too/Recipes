Ingredients

Bagel Dough (60% hydration)

    8 g Instant Yeast

    20 g Honey (or sugar)

    300 g Water (~105 F)

    500 g Bread Flour (preferably)

    10 g Salt

    Cornmeal for sprinkling on baking sheet

Poaching liquid

    ~ 2 liters (quarts) of Water

    15 g Baking Soda

    15 g Salt

    30 g Honey (or barley malt syrup or sugar)

Egg wash

    1 Egg

    15 ml Water

Bake at 246c for 12 minutes.
