The Ingredients:

Milk: 400g (Use this as the only liquid; omit the water)
Flour: 510g (approx. 4 ¼ cups)
Sugar: 20g (1 ½ tablespoons)
Salt: 12g (2 teaspoons)
Baking Soda: ¼ teaspoon (heaping)
Instant Yeast: 12g (approx. 4 teaspoons or 1 ½ packets)
Vegetable Oil: 35g (2 ½ tablespoons)
Cornmeal: For dusting the pan


Important Equipment Change

Pan Size: The original recipe fits in a standard 8.5" x 4.5" pan. This new batch is 40% larger. You must use a 9" x 5" loaf pan (the larger standard size). If you use the smaller pan, the batter will overflow in the oven.

Key Changes to the Process

Heating the Milk: Since you are heating pure milk (rather than a water/milk mix), be careful not to scald it. Heat it to 120°F–130°F. If you don't have a thermometer, it should feel hot to the touch—hotter than lukewarm bathwater, but not so hot that you have to pull your finger away immediately.

Browning: Because you are using 100% milk (which has more milk sugar/lactose) instead of a water/milk blend, the crust will brown faster. Keep an eye on the oven around the 20-minute mark. If the top gets too dark before the inside hits 190°F, tent a piece of foil over the top loosely.

Mixing: The method remains the same: Mix dry ingredients, pour in hot liquid, beat on high for 1 minute (or hand beat for 3 mins).

Rising: In a 9x5 pan, let it rise until the dough is just barely crowning over the rim (about 1/4 inch over). Do not let it rise too high, or it might collapse when baking.
