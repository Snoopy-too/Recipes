Kansai-Style Yakitori Tare (関西風焼き鳥のタレ)

A clean, balanced tare glaze with a rounded sweetness (*maroyaka*), optimized for easy home cooking.

# Ingredients

- **Soy sauce (*****koikuchi*****):** 100 ml (\~7 tbsp)
- **Mirin (*****hon-mirin*****):** 80 ml (\~5.5 tbsp)
- **Japanese cooking sake:** 80 ml (\~5.5 tbsp)
- **Sugar:** 35–40 g (\~2.5–3 tbsp)*(Coarse/zarame or raw sugar works best; standard granulated sugar is fine)*
- **Green onion tops (*****ao-negi*****):** 1 piece (\~5–7 cm)
- **Fresh ginger:** 1 thin slice (\~2 mm)

### Quick Umami Boost (Bone-Free Options)

- **Chicken bouillon / Torigara soup stock powder:** 1/4 tsp (dissolve directly into the pot)
- *Or* **Charred negi:** Blister the green onion tops over a flame before adding to add smoky depth.

## Instructions

| Step | Stage | Action Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | **Boil Off Alcohol** | In a small saucepan, combine sake and mirin. Bring to a boil over medium heat for 1–2 minutes. |
| **2** | **Combine & Season** | Lower heat to medium-low. Stir in soy sauce, sugar, and optional chicken bouillon powder until dissolved. Add green onion and ginger. |
| **3** | **Simmer & Reduce** | Simmer gently for 12–18 minutes, stirring occasionally, until reduced by 1/3 to 1/2.  *(The sauce should lightly coat the back of a spoon; it thickens further as it cools).* |
| **4** | **Cool & Strain** | Discard the green onion and ginger. Let cool completely before transferring to a clean jar. |

## How to Glaze Yakitori

- Grill skewers over medium-high heat until **70–80% cooked through**.
- Dip or brush with tare.
- Grill briefly to caramelize the sauce.
- Brush once more right before pulling off the grill.

## Storage

- Store in a sealed airtight jar in the refrigerator for up to **2–3 months**.
