Authentic Japanese Curry

**Doubled Recipe — Weighted in Grams****Yield:** 8–10 servings | **Base:** Roux-from-scratch (Curry powder)

| Attribute | Details |

## Ingredients

### Main Ingredients

- **Thinly sliced beef (komagire):** 500 g
- **Potatoes:** 400–600 g *(approx. 3–4 medium, peeled & cut into bite-sized pieces)*
- **Onions:** 600–800 g *(approx. 3–4 medium, thinly sliced)*
- **Carrot:** 200 g *(approx. 1 medium, cut into bite-sized pieces)*

### Curry Roux Base

- **Japanese curry powder:** 24 g *(approx. 4 level tbsp / slightly over one 23 g Hachi pouch)*
- **All-purpose flour:** 54–72 g *(6–8 tbsp)*

### Seasonings & Liquids

- **Neutral cooking oil:** 26–52 g *(2–4 tbsp)*
- **Garlic paste (from tube):** 16 g *(approx. 4–6 cm squeezed)*
- **Water:** 1,400 g *(1,400 ml)*
- **Bouillon / Consommé cubes:** 10.6 g *(2 standard cubes / 5.3 g each)*
- **Ketchup:** 36–72 g *(2–4 tbsp)*
- **Japanese Worcestershire sauce:** 36 g *(2 tbsp)*
- **Soy sauce:** 36 g *(2 tbsp)*
- **Granulated sugar:** 8 g *(2 tsp)*
- **Salt:** 10–12 g *(approx. 2 tsp, adjust to taste)*
- **Black/white pepper:** 0.5–1 g *(a few generous dashes)*
- **Whole milk:** 62 g *(approx. 4 tbsp / 60 ml)*

## Instructions

### Step 1: Preparation

- Thinly slice **600–800 g onions**.
- Peel and cut **400–600 g potatoes** and **200 g carrots** into bite-sized chunks.

### Step 2: Sauté the Aromatics & Meat

- Place a large heavy-bottomed pot on the scale or tare your pan, then heat **26–52 g cooking oil** over medium heat.
- Add the sliced onions and sauté for about **10 minutes** until caramelized and lightly browned.
- Add **16 g garlic paste** and **500 g beef**. Cook until the meat changes color.
- Add the potatoes and carrots; stir for 1–2 minutes to coat in the seasoned oil.

### Step 3: Toast the Spices & Flour

- Stir in **24 g curry powder** and cook for 30–60 seconds until deeply fragrant.
- Lower the heat to low, add **54–72 g flour**, and stir constantly for about **1 minute** to cook out the raw flour flavor without scorching.

### Step 4: Simmer

- Gradually add **1,400 g water** in two batches, stirring thoroughly to incorporate the roux smoothly without forming lumps.
- Add **10.6 g bouillon cubes (2 cubes)**.
- Cover with a lid and simmer over **low to medium-low heat for 15 minutes**, until the vegetables are fork-tender.

### Step 5: Season & Finish

- Stir in:
    - **36–72 g ketchup**
    - **36 g Worcestershire sauce**
    - **36 g soy sauce**
    - **8 g sugar**
    - **0.5–1 g pepper**
- Simmer uncovered for about **3 minutes**, stirring gently until the sauce thickens to your liking.
- Finish by stirring in **62 g milk** and seasoning with **10–12 g salt** *(adjust to taste)*.
- Serve hot with freshly steamed Japanese rice.
