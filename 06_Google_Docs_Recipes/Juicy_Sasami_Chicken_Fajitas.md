# Juicy Sasami (Chicken Tenderloin) Fajitas Guide

Chicken tenderloins (**sasami** / ささみ) make exceptionally tender, quick-cooking fajitas. Because sasami is lean, retaining moisture requires proper tendon preparation, an oil-and-acid-based marinade, and brief, controlled high-heat cooking.

## 1. Meat Preparation: Handling & Tendon Removal

Sasami contains a tough white tendon (*suji*) running along the underside. Removing this ensures every strip remains tender.

- **Safe Thawing:** Thaw the frozen sasami overnight in the refrigerator (or sealed in a watertight bag submerged in cold water for quick thawing). Pat completely dry with paper towels.
- **Tendon Extraction (Fork Method):** Grip the protruding white tip of the tendon with a paper towel for traction. Place the tines of a fork over the tendon, hold the meat firmly against a cutting board, and pull the tendon upward and through the tines.
- **Tendon Extraction (Knife Method):** Place the tenderloin tendon-side down. Make a small slit around the tendon tip to grab it, then slide the flat blade of a chef's knife along the tendon at a slight angle, scraping the meat away.
- **Slicing:** Cut each tenderloin diagonally into 1.5 cm to 2 cm wide strips across the grain to maintain juiciness and promote fast searing.

## 2. Fajita Marinade & Measurements

The marinade provides flavor, introduces healthy fats to insulate lean meat, and uses citrus acid to lightly tenderize the chicken fibers.

| **Ingredient** | **Amount (500g Sasami)** | **Amount (1kg Sasami)** | **Function / Role** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Olive Oil (or neutral high-heat oil) | 2 tbsp (30 ml) | 4 tbsp (60 ml) | Coats meat to prevent drying out and helps spices adhere |
| Fresh Lime Juice | 1.5 tbsp (about 1 lime) | 3 tbsp (about 2 limes) | Acidic tenderizer and bright citrus balance |
| Garlic (minced or grated) | 3 cloves | 6 cloves | Aromatic base |
| Ground Cumin | 1 tsp | 2 tsp | Warm, earthy Mexican profile |
| Smoked Paprika | 1 tsp | 2 tsp | Color and smoky grill-like depth |
| Chili Powder | 1 tsp | 2 tsp | Gentle warmth and depth |
| Dried Oregano | 1/2 tsp | 1 tsp | Herbal complexity |
| Fine Salt & Black Pepper | 1 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp pepper | 2 tsp salt, 1 tsp pepper | Essential seasoning |

**Marinating Time:** 20 to 30 minutes at room temperature. Avoid marinating tender sasami for more than 2 hours, as excessive lime exposure can break down the delicate muscle fibers and turn the meat mushy.

## 3. Cooking Methods Without Cast Iron

| **Method** | **Temperature / Heat** | **Total Cook Time** | **Key Technique** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Non-Stick Frying Pan** | Medium-High | 3 to 4 minutes per batch | Cook in small batches; cook veggies and chicken separately to prevent stewing. |
| **Air Fryer** | 200°C (400°F) | 6 to 8 minutes | Single-layer placement; shake basket at 4 minutes for even browning. |
| **Oven (Sheet Pan Broiler)** | 220°C (425°F) or High Broil | 10 to 12 minutes | Preheat the baking sheet inside the oven so food sizzles upon contact. |

### Method A: Non-Stick Frying Pan (Recommended for Best Control)

- **Cook Vegetables First:** Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in the non-stick pan over medium-high heat. Add sliced bell peppers and onions. Sauté undisturbed for 2 minutes to get char marks, then toss frequently for another 3 to 4 minutes until crisp-tender. Transfer to a bowl.
- **Sear Chicken in Batches:** Wipe excess marinade from the chicken strips (excess liquid prevents browning). Add 1 teaspoon of oil to the pan. Lay strips in a single layer with space between them (cook 500g in 2 batches).
- **Flash Cook:** Let sit undisturbed for 90 seconds to develop light caramelization. Flip and cook for an additional 90 to 120 seconds until just cooked through (internal temperature reaches 74°C / 165°F). Transfer to a warm plate immediately.
- **Combine & Reheat:** Return the cooked vegetables and all chicken batches to the pan for 30 seconds over low heat just to mingle the juices.

### Method B: Air Fryer (Easiest Char)

- Preheat the air fryer to 200°C (400°F) for 3 minutes.
- Arrange the marinated sasami strips in a single layer in the air fryer basket (do not overlap).
- Air fry at 200°C for 6 to 8 minutes, shaking the basket at the 4-minute mark.
- Air fry sliced peppers and onions separately at 200°C for 8 to 10 minutes, shaking halfway through.

### Method C: Oven Sheet Pan

- Place a heavy rimmed baking sheet on the upper rack of the oven and preheat the oven to 220°C (425°F).
- Toss sliced peppers and onions with 1 tbsp oil, salt, and pepper.
- Carefully remove the hot baking sheet, spread the vegetables and marinated chicken strips across the sheet in a single layer.
- Bake for 10 to 12 minutes, or switch to High Broil for the final 2 to 3 minutes for blistered edges.

## 4. Assembly & Accompaniments

- **Warm Tortillas:** Warm flour or corn tortillas directly in a dry non-stick skillet for 20 seconds per side, or stack and wrap in damp paper towels and microwave for 30 seconds.
- **Recommended Garnishes:** Fresh cilantro, lime wedges, sliced avocado or guacamole, sour cream (or Greek yogurt), salsa / pico de gallo, and shredded cheese.
