#  Classic Southern Buttermilk Fried Chicken (Drumelets)

**Mobile-Friendly Quick View**

Classic Southern chicken drumelets marinated in spiced buttermilk and dredged in seasoned flour & cornstarch for a crunchy crust and juicy meat.

## Quick Specs

- **Prep Time:** 20 mins
- **Marination:** 2–8 hours (or overnight)
- **Cook Time:** 8–10 mins per batch
- **Oil Temp:** 175°C (350°F)
- **Target Internal Temp:** 74°C (165°F)
- **Yield:** 3–4 servings

## Ingredients

### 1. Chicken & Marinade

- **1.0 kg (2.2 lbs)** Chicken drumelets
- **360 ml (1.5 cups)** Buttermilk (or whole milk + 1.5 tbsp lemon juice/vinegar)
- **1.5 tbsp (22 ml)** Hot sauce
- **¾ tsp (4.5 g)** Fine salt
- **1 large** Egg (whisked in)

### 2. Seasoned Flour Dredge

- **185 g (1.5 cups)** All-purpose flour
- **45 g (⅓ cup)** Cornstarch / Katakuriko
- **2 tsp (8 g)** Baking powder
- **2 tsp (5 g)** Paprika (sweet or smoked)
- **2 tsp (6 g)** Garlic powder
- **2 tsp (5 g)** Onion powder
- **2 tsp (12 g)** Fine salt
- **¾ tsp (1.8 g)** Black pepper
- **¾ tsp (1.5 g)** Cayenne pepper

### 3. Frying Medium

- **750 ml – 1 L** Neutral oil (canola, vegetable, peanut; 4–5 cm / 1.5–2 in depth)

## Step-by-Step Instructions

- **Marinate:** Whisk buttermilk, hot sauce, salt, and egg. Submerge drumelets; cover and chill 2–8 hours (or overnight).
- **Prepare Dredge:** Whisk flour, cornstarch, baking powder, and spices. Drizzle 2–3 tbsp buttermilk marinade into the flour and toss with a fork to form small craggy clusters.
- **Bread & Rest:** Coat drumelets thoroughly in flour mix, pressing firmly into crevices. Rest on wire rack for 10–15 mins at room temperature.
- **Heat Oil:** Heat oil in a deep heavy skillet to 175°C (350°F).
- **Fry:** Fry in small batches without overcrowding. Maintain oil at 155°C–165°C (310°F–325°F). Fry drumelets for 8–10 mins, turning every 2–3 mins.
- **Check & Drain:** Verify 74°C (165°F) internal temp at bone. Drain on wire rack over a tray and sprinkle immediately with fine salt.
- **Repeat & Hold:** Bring oil back to 175°C between batches. Keep warm in 95°C (200°F) oven.

## Dinner Timing Tip (with Baked Bareisho Potatoes)

- **Bake Potatoes First:** Bake scrubbed, oiled, and salted potatoes at 200°C (400°F) for 50–60 mins.
- **Start Frying at 15 Mins Left:** Begin frying drumelets when potatoes have 15 mins left to finish together.
