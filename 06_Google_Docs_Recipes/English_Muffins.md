English Muffins

A simple, delicious English muffin recipe optimized for easy reading on mobile devices.

## 📋 Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **550 g** | Bread flour | Strong |
| **430 g** | Whole milk | Lukewarm |
| **35 g** | Unsalted butter | Softened |
| **15 g** | Sugar |  |
| **11 g** | Fine salt |  |
| **6 g** | Instant yeast |  |
| **30 g** | Semolina or cornmeal | For dusting |

## 🥣 Instructions

1. **Mix the Dough**
    - In a large mixing bowl or stand mixer, combine the lukewarm milk, sugar, and instant yeast.
    - Add the bread flour, salt, and softened butter.
    - Mix until a soft, slightly sticky dough forms.
1. **Knead**
    - Knead for about 8–10 minutes (by hand or with a dough hook on medium-low) until smooth, elastic, and stretchy.
1. **First Rise**
    - Place dough in a lightly oiled bowl, cover, and let rise in a warm spot for about 1 to 1.5 hours, or until doubled in size.
1. **Shape**
    - Dust a baking sheet generously with half of the semolina/cornmeal.
    - Turn dough onto a lightly floured surface and divide into 8 to 10 equal portions.
    - Roll each piece into a smooth ball, then flatten gently into a disk (about 2.5 cm / 1 inch thick).
    - Place disks onto the dusted sheet and sprinkle tops with the remaining semolina/cornmeal.
1. **Second Proof**
    - Cover loosely and let rest for 30–45 minutes until slightly puffy.
1. **Cook on Griddle / Skillet**
    - Heat a dry, heavy skillet or cast-iron pan over medium-low heat.
    - Cook muffins in batches for about 6–8 minutes per side, until deep golden brown and cooked through (internal temperature around 93°C / 200°F).
    - Transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.

## 💡 Pro Tip

- Fork-split muffins around the edges rather than slicing with a knife to preserve those classic nooks and crannies!
