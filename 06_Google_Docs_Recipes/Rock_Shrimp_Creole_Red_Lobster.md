Rock Shrimp Creole (Red Lobster)

**Servings:** Serves 6–8**Description:** Rock shrimp meets Creole cuisine in a spicy sauce with tomatoes, onions, and peppers. Dip your favorite bread into a big bite of Louisiana.

# Ingredients (in Grams / Metric)

| Ingredient | Amount (Grams) | Notes / Traditional Measure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rock shrimp (peeled and deveined) | 907 g |  |
| Butter (Portion 1) | 28.5 g |  |
| Butter (Portion 2) | 28.5 g |  |
| Green bell pepper | 75 g |  |
| Onion | 80 g |  |
| Celery | 30 g |  |
| Garlic | 3 g |  |
| All-purpose flour | 16 g |  |
| Canned whole peeled tomatoes (with juice) | 411 g |  |
| Granulated sugar | 4 g |  |
| Salt | 6 g |  |
| Cayenne pepper | 0.6 g |  |
| Black pepper | 0.6 g |  |
| Cooked rice | 500–600 g |  |

## Preparation

1. **Clean Shrimp:** Rinse shrimp briefly and remove any large veins that are visible.
1. **Melt Butter:** In a small saucepan, melt 28.5 g of butter over medium heat.
1. **Make Roux:** Mix in 16 g of flour and stir until dissolved and mixture begins to thicken, then reduce heat.
1. **Add Tomatoes:** Add 411 g of tomatoes and their juice, sugar (4 g), salt (6 g), cayenne pepper (0.6 g), and black pepper (0.6 g) to the butter and flour mixture, stir well, and heat through. The mixture will thicken. Hold warm.
1. **Sauté Vegetables:** In a large skillet, melt the remaining 28.5 g of butter. Over medium-high heat, cook the diced bell pepper (75 g), onion (80 g), celery (30 g), and minced garlic (3 g) until soft, but with a little bit of a crunch.
1. **Cook Shrimp:** Add the 907 g of shrimp and cook until no longer transparent (approx. 3 minutes).
1. **Simmer:** Add the warm tomato mixture, bring to a boil, cover, and let simmer for 5 minutes.
1. **Serve:** Serve hot over 500–600 g of warm rice (white or wild rice mixture).

## Chef's Tips

- **Cooked Shrimp Variation:** If rock shrimp are only available precooked, add the shrimp and the tomato mixture at the same time.
- **Extra Flavor:** Add Cajun-style sausage when cooking the vegetables for an extra Cajun flair.
- **Seasoning Options:** Cajun- or Louisiana-style seasoning can be used instead of, or along with, salt, cayenne pepper, and black pepper.
- **Serving Suggestion:** Serve with warm French or sourdough bread.
