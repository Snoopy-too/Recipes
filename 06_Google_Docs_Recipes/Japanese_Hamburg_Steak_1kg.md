Japanese Hamburg Steak (1 kg Batch)

Yield: 8 large patties (\~185 g each) or 10 standard patties (\~150 g each)

# 1. Onion Preparation

- Yellow onions: 800 g (approx. 4 medium onions, finely diced)
- Cooking oil: 20 g


Directions:Heat oil in a skillet over medium heat. Fry diced onions until soft, translucent, and lightly browned (yields approx. 495 g cooked onion). Let cool completely.


- 330 g cooked onion -> reserved for Meat Mixture
- 165 g cooked onion -> reserved for Onion Gravy

## 2. Meat Mixture (Patties)

Ingredients:


- Ground beef & pork mix (7:3 ratio): 1,000 g (700 g beef, 300 g pork)
- Salt: 8 g
- Black pepper: 1.5 g
- Ground nutmeg: 4.5 g
- Prepared fried onion: 330 g (cooled)
- Panko breadcrumbs: 60 g
- Milk: 124 g
- Eggs: 200 g (4 large eggs)


Directions:


1. Soak Panko: In a small bowl, combine 60 g panko and 124 g milk. Let sit for 2–3 minutes until soft.
1. Knead Meat: In a large chilled bowl, add the 1,000 g meat mixture and 8 g salt. Knead vigorously with cold hands until pale and sticky.
1. Combine: Add black pepper (1.5 g), nutmeg (4.5 g), soaked panko, eggs (200 g), and cooled fried onions (330 g). Mix thoroughly until evenly incorporated.
1. Shape: Divide into 8 to 10 equal oval patties. Slap each patty back and forth between your hands 5–6 times to expel air pockets. Press a shallow indentation into the center of each patty.

## 3. Cooking the Patties

- Cooking oil: 15 g


Directions:


1. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
1. Place patties in skillet. Sear for 2–3 minutes until a rich golden-brown crust forms.
1. Flip patties, reduce heat to low, cover with a tight-fitting lid, and steam-cook for 7–8 minutes until cooked through (internal temp 74°C / 165°F).
1. Transfer patties to a warm plate and keep skillet juices for the gravy.

## 4. Onion Gravy

Ingredients:


- Prepared fried onion: 165 g
- Skillet pan drippings: retained from cooking patties
- Water: 200 g
- Soy sauce: 105 g
- Mirin: 75 g
- Grated garlic: 18 g
- Butter: 40 g


Directions:


1. In the same skillet with pan drippings, add the 165 g reserved fried onions, 200 g water, 105 g soy sauce, 75 g mirin, and 18 g grated garlic.
1. Bring to a gentle simmer over medium heat, scraping up browned bits from the pan. Simmer for 2–3 minutes until slightly thickened.
1. Turn off heat. Stir in 40 g butter until melted and glossy.
1. Spoon hot gravy generously over the hamburg patties and serve.
