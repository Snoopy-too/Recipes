# Mexican-Style Refried Beans (Canned Mixed Beans)

A quick, flavorful, and hearty recipe adapted specifically for a 400g can of mixed beans (such as borlotti, kidney, white beans, chickpeas, and butter beans). Perfect as a side dish for tacos, enchiladas, or served with tortilla chips.

| **Prep Time** | **Cook Time** | **Total Time** | **Servings** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 mins | 10–12 mins | 15–17 mins | 2–3 servings |

## Ingredients

- **1 can (400g / 240g drained)** Mixed beans (canned in water/brine)
- **1.5 to 2 tbsp** Cooking fat (lard, bacon drippings, or neutral oil like avocado/canola)
- **1/4 medium** Yellow or white onion, finely diced
- **2 cloves** Fresh garlic, finely minced or grated
- **1/2 tsp** Ground cumin
- **1/4 tsp** Chili powder or smoked paprika
- **1/4 tsp** Dried oregano (preferably Mexican oregano)
- **2–4 tbsp** Reserved bean liquid, vegetable broth, or water (as needed for texture)
- **Salt & black pepper** to taste

## Instructions

- **Prep the Beans:** Open the can of mixed beans over a bowl and strain the beans, reserving 3 to 4 tablespoons of the canning liquid for later. Give the beans a light rinse if desired.
- **Sauté Aromatics:** Place a skillet or medium saucepan over medium heat. Melt the lard or heat your cooking oil. Add the finely diced onion and cook for 3 to 4 minutes until softened and translucent. Add the minced garlic and cook for about 1 minute until aromatic, ensuring the garlic does not brown.
- **Bloom Spices:** Add the ground cumin, chili powder (or paprika), and dried oregano into the oil. Stir constantly for 30 seconds to allow the essential oils and flavors to bloom.
- **Add Beans & Simmer:** Pour the drained mixed beans into the skillet along with 2 tablespoons of the reserved bean liquid or broth. Stir well and bring the mixture to a gentle simmer for 2 to 3 minutes until the beans are heated through and slightly softened.
- **Mash to Desired Consistency:** Reduce the heat to medium-low. Using a potato masher, the back of a sturdy fork, or a wooden spoon, mash the beans directly in the pan. Continue mashing until you reach your preferred texture. If the mixture feels too dry or thick, splash in more of the reserved liquid or broth 1 tablespoon at a time.
- **Finish & Season:** Simmer for another 2 to 3 minutes while stirring frequently until the beans pull away slightly from the sides of the pan. Taste and adjust seasoning with salt and freshly cracked black pepper. Remove from heat.

## Chef's Tips & Texture Adjustments

- **Handling Chickpeas & Butter Beans:** Because mixed cans contain firmer beans like garbanzo (chickpeas), hand-mashing will give a rustic, chunky consistency. For an ultra-smooth, restaurant-style velvety texture, pulse the beans briefly in a food processor or with an immersion blender before simmering.
- **Fat Choice:** Traditional Mexican refried beans get much of their signature savory richness from pork lard (*manteca*) or bacon grease. For a vegetarian/vegan version, use neutral oil or olive oil and consider adding a dash of smoked paprika for extra depth.
- **Serving Suggestions:** Garnish hot with crumbled cotija or queso fresco (or shredded Monterey Jack/cheddar), chopped fresh cilantro, a squeeze of fresh lime juice, or sliced pickled jalapeños.

## Storage & Reheating

- **Refrigerate:** Store in an airtight container for up to 4 to 5 days.
- **Reheat:** The starch in beans will cause them to firm up when cooled. When reheating in a skillet or microwave, add a splash of warm water, broth, or a touch of butter/oil to restore the creamy texture.
