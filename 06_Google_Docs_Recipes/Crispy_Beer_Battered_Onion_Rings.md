Ultimate Crispy Beer-Battered Onion Rings

*Light, extra-crunchy onion rings with a deeply seasoned golden batter that stays crisp.*


| Prep Time | Cook Time | Total Time | Servings | Difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 15 mins | 15 mins | 30 mins | 4 servings | Easy |

# Ingredients

### For the Onion Rings

- **2 large (approx. 500g)** Vidalia or Sweet Yellow Onions (sliced into ½-inch thick rounds)
- **1 quart (946 ml)** Neutral oil with a high smoke point (Vegetable, Peanut, or Canola)

### Dry Dredge & Batter

- **1 cup (120g)** All-Purpose Flour
- **½ cup (64g)** Cornstarch *(essential for lightweight crunch)*
- **1 tsp (4g)** Baking Powder
- **1 tsp (2.3g)** Smoked Paprika
- **1 tsp (3g)** Garlic Powder
- **½ tsp (1.2g)** Onion Powder
- **½ tsp (0.9g)** Cayenne Pepper *(optional)*
- **1 tsp (6g)** Kosher Salt
- **½ tsp (1.2g)** Freshly Ground Black Pepper
- **1 cup (240ml)** Cold Beer *(Lager, Pilsner, or Pale Ale work best)*

### Spicy Comeback Dipping Sauce

- **½ cup (115g)** Mayonnaise
- **2 tbsp (34g)** Ketchup
- **1 tbsp (15g)** Sriracha or Hot Sauce
- **1 tsp (5g)** Worcestershire Sauce
- **¼ tsp (0.8g)** Garlic Powder

## Instructions

### 1. Prepare the Onions

1. Peel the outer skin of the onions and slice horizontally into ½-inch thick rounds.
1. Carefully separate the rounds into individual rings. Reserve the tiny inner core for another recipe.
1. **Crucial Step:** Peel off the thin inner papery membrane on the inside of each ring. This prevents the onion from sliding out of the batter when you take a bite!

### 2. Set Up the Dredging Station

1. In a large bowl, whisk together the **flour**, **cornstarch**, **baking powder**, **smoked paprika**, **garlic powder**, **onion powder**, **cayenne**, **salt**, and **black pepper**.
1. Scoop out **½ cup (60g)** of this dry flour mixture and place it on a separate shallow plate (this will be used for the initial dry coat).
1. Slowly pour the **cold beer** into the remaining flour mix in the main bowl. Whisk gently until just combined. *Do not overmix; small lumps are fine, and carbonation keeps the batter light.*

### 3. Batter and Fry

1. Heat 2 inches of oil in a heavy Dutch oven or deep fryer to **375°F (190°C)**.
1. Working in small batches of 4 to 5 rings:
    - Coat each ring thoroughly in the **dry flour mix**, shaking off any excess.
    - Dip into the **beer batter**, making sure it is fully coated, then let excess batter drip back into the bowl for 2 seconds.
1. Gently lower the rings into the hot oil. Fry for **2 to 3 minutes**, turning halfway through, until deep golden brown and crispy.
1. Transfer fried rings to a wire rack set over a baking sheet (avoid using paper towels, as steam gets trapped and turns the bottom soggy).
1. Immediately sprinkle with a light pinch of extra kosher salt while hot.

## Pro Tips for Perfection

- **Ice-Cold Beer:** Cold batter reacting with hot oil creates steam bubbles that form an extraordinarily light, bubbly crust.
- **Maintain Oil Temp:** Do not overcrowd the pot, as this drops the oil temperature and produces greasy onion rings.
- **Keep Warm:** Keep finished batches warm in an oven preheated to **200°F (95°C)** on a wire rack while frying the rest.
