# Classic Southern Buttermilk Fried Chicken (Boneless Thighs)

**Mobile-Friendly Quick View**

Adapted for boneless chicken thighs (cutlets/nuggets). Spiced buttermilk marinade with a double-dredged crunchy crust.

## Quick Specs

- **Prep Time:** 15–20 mins
- **Marination:** 1–4 hours (up to 8 hrs)
- **Cook Time:** 6–8 mins per batch
- **Oil Temp:** 175°C (350°F)
- **Target Internal Temp:** 74°C (165°F)
- **Yield:** 4–6 servings

## Ingredients

### 1. Chicken & Marinade

- **800 g – 1.2 kg** Boneless chicken thighs (halved/palm-sized pieces, 70–90g each)
- **480 ml (2 cups)** Buttermilk (or milk + 2 tbsp lemon juice/vinegar, rested 10 min)
- **2 tbsp (30 ml)** Hot sauce (Tabasco, Frank's, or cayenne-based)
- **1 tsp (6 g)** Fine salt
- **1 large** Egg (whisked in)

### 2. Seasoned Flour Dredge

- **250 g (2 cups)** All-purpose flour
- **60 g (½ cup)** Cornstarch / Katakuriko
- **1 tbsp (12 g)** Baking powder
- **1 tbsp (7 g)** Paprika (sweet or smoked)
- **1 tbsp (9 g)** Garlic powder
- **1 tbsp (7 g)** Onion powder
- **1 tbsp (18 g)** Fine salt
- **1 tsp (2.5 g)** Freshly cracked black pepper
- **1 tsp (2 g)** Cayenne pepper

### 3. Frying Medium

- **1 – 1.5 L** Neutral oil (canola, vegetable, peanut, corn; 4–5 cm / 1.5–2 in deep)

## Key Boneless Thigh Adjustments

- **Portioning:** Cut whole thighs in halves or thirds for even cooking.
- **Shorter Cook Time:** 6–8 minutes total (versus 12–15 for bone-in).
- **Faster Marinating:** 1–2 hours is plenty (up to 8 hours max).

## Step-by-Step Instructions

- **Prep & Marinate:** Trim excess fat from thighs and cut into halves. Whisk buttermilk, hot sauce, salt, and egg in a bowl. Submerge chicken; chill 1–2 hours (up to 8 hours).
- **Mix Dredge & Form Flakes:** Whisk flour, cornstarch, baking powder, and all seasonings in a shallow dish. Drizzle 3–4 tbsp marinade into the flour and toss with a fork to make craggy clusters.
- **Dredge & Rest:** Lift chicken from marinade, let excess drip off. Coat and press firmly into flour mix. Rest on a wire rack for 10–15 minutes at room temp so coating sets.
- **Heat Oil:** Heat oil in deep pan to 175°C (350°F).
- **Fry:** Fry 3–4 pieces at a time for 6–8 minutes (flipping halfway) until deep golden and internal temp hits 74°C (165°F).
- **Drain & Season:** Move to wire rack over a tray. Sprinkle with fine salt immediately while hot.
- **Repeat:** Reheat oil to 175°C before next batch. Hold finished pieces in 95°C (200°F) oven if desired.

## Dinner Timing Tip (with Baked Bareisho Potatoes)

- **Bake Potatoes First:** Scrub potatoes, poke with fork, rub with oil and salt. Bake at 200°C (400°F) for 50–60 mins.
- **Start Frying at 10 Mins Left:** Drop chicken when potatoes have 10–12 mins left so both finish hot together.
