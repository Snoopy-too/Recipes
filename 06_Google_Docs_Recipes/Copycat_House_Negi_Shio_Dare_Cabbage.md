# Copycat House Negi Shio Dare Cabbage

**Mobile-Friendly Quick View (600g & 700g Batch)**

Gram-accurate copycat recipe replicating House Spice Cooking Negi Shio Dare Cabbage for a kitchen scale.

## Quick Specs

- **Base Weight:** 600.0 g raw green cabbage
- **Prep Time:** 3 mins
- **Rest Time:** 5–10 mins
- **Flavor:** Savory chicken bouillon, toasted sesame, garlic, black pepper, scallion

## Gram-Accurate Ingredients (600g Cabbage)

- **600.0 g** Fresh cabbage leaves (\~12–14 large leaves)
- **60.0 g (\~4.5 tbsp)** Pure toasted sesame oil (ごま油)
- **6.0 g (2 tsp)** Chicken bouillon granules / powder (鶏がらスープの素)
- **3.5 g (\~0.6 tsp)** Fine salt (食塩)
- **2.8 g (1 tsp)** Garlic powder (ガーリックパウダー) or 6.0g fresh grated
- **6.0 g (1 tsp)** Japanese soy sauce (濃口しょうゆ)
- **2.0 g (0.5 tsp)** White granulated sugar (上白糖 / グラニュー糖)
- **12.0 g (\~1.5 tbsp)** Roasted white sesame seeds (いりごま 白)
- **2.5 g** Dried green scallions / negi (乾燥ねぎ) or 20g fresh minced
- **1.2 g (\~0.5 tsp)** Coarse ground black pepper (あらびき黒こしょう)
- **0.3 g (1 pinch)** Ichimi togarashi (一味唐辛子)
- **0.5 g (2 shakes)** MSG / Ajinomoto (味の素) \[Optional\]

## Step-by-Step Instructions

- **Prep Cabbage:** Wash and thoroughly dry cabbage. Remove thick cores or slice thinly. Tear leaves into 4–5 cm bite-sized squares.
- **Weigh & Whisk Dressing:** Place a large bowl on the scale, tare to 0.0g, and weigh all seasonings directly into the bowl. Whisk well to dissolve salt and bouillon granules.
- **Toss:** Add cabbage in 2–3 batches. Toss thoroughly until all leaves are evenly glossy.
- **Rest & Serve:** Rest at room temp for 5–10 minutes to lightly wilt cabbage, then serve.

## Full 700g Batch Method (Using 1 Commercial Packet)

- Tear 700.0 g cabbage.
- Combine 1 commercial House packet (3.4 g) + 13.5 g (1 tbsp) sesame oil.
- Add the full copycat blend above (60.0 g sesame oil + copycat seasonings).
- Toss together until evenly coated.
