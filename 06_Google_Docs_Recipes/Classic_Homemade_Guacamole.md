# Classic Homemade Guacamole Recipe

A fresh, authentic recipe designed for rich texture and balanced flavor. Optimized for mobile kitchen viewing.

## Recipe Overview

---

| **Detail** | **Information** |
| --- | --- |
| **Prep Time** | 15 minutes |
| **Cook Time** | 0 minutes |
| **Yield** | 4 to 6 servings |
| **Texture Profile** | Chunky & rustic |

## Ingredients

---

| **Ingredient** | **Quantity** | **Preparation Notes** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hass Avocados** | 3 ripe | Yields slightly to gentle thumb pressure |
| **White Onion** | 1/2 medium (\~1/2 cup) | Finely diced |
| **Jalapeño or Serrano** | 1 to 2 fresh | Seeded & finely minced (keep seeds for heat) |
| **Fresh Cilantro** | 1/4 cup | Finely chopped (leaves & tender stems) |
| **Roma Tomato** | 1 medium | Seeded and diced (optional, for texture) |
| **Fresh Lime Juice** | 1 to 2 tbsp | Freshly squeezed (\~1 juicy lime) |
| **Coarse Sea Salt** | 1/2 to 3/4 tsp | To taste (kosher salt works well) |
| **Garlic (Optional)** | 1 small clove | Finely grated or mashed into a paste |

## Step-by-Step Instructions

- ---**Build the Aromatic Base (The Traditional Step):**In a molcajete or wide mixing bowl, combine the diced onion, minced chile, half of the chopped cilantro, and 1/2 teaspoon of coarse salt. Lightly mash with a pestle or the back of a fork against the bowl sides. This releases aromatic oils and creates an infused flavor base throughout the guacamole.
- **Prep and Mash the Avocados:**Cut avocados in half lengthwise, remove pits, and scoop flesh into the bowl. Coarsely mash using a fork or potato masher. Leave distinct, bite-sized avocado chunks rather than creating a smooth purée.
- **Fold and Incorporate:**Add the fresh lime juice, diced tomato (if using), and remaining cilantro. Gently fold the ingredients together with a spatula or spoon until just combined.
- **Taste and Calibrate:**Sample with a tortilla chip (since salted chips affect final flavor). Add extra salt or lime juice in small increments until the flavors taste bright and full.

## Chef's Tips & Storage

### ---

Preventing Browning

If not serving right away, smooth the top and press plastic wrap directly against the surface of the guacamole so no air is trapped against it, then chill in the refrigerator. Physical exposure to oxygen is what causes rapid oxidation.


Texture Control

Never use a food processor or blender. High speed whips air into avocado fat, resulting in a pale, foamy, watery texture instead of a rich, velvety consistency.
