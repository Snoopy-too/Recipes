Lemon-Zest & Fresh Basil Olive Oil Drizzle

*Ideal pairing for fresh sliced tomatoes and fresh mozzarella.*

# Yield & Scaling

- **Yield:** Generously coats 3 large tomatoes and approx. 300g fresh mozzarella (approx. 4–5 servings).

## Ingredients

- **Extra virgin olive oil:** 5–6 tbsp / approx. 70–82 g (approx. 1/3 cup)
- **Fresh lemon juice:** 2–3 tsp / approx. 10–15 g
- **Lemon zest:** Zest of 1 full lemon (finely grated)
- **Fresh basil:** 2–3 tbsp, finely sliced (chiffonade) or lightly torn
- **Red pepper flakes:** 1/4 tsp (optional, for subtle warmth)
- **Flaky sea salt:** To taste
- **Freshly cracked black pepper:** To taste

## Instructions

1. **Prep the Platter:**
    - Slice the fresh tomatoes and mozzarella into even rounds.
    - Arrange them alternating across your serving platter.
    - Lightly sprinkle the tomato slices with flaky sea salt. Let them sit for 2–3 minutes to begin releasing their natural juices.
1. **Whisk the Drizzle:**
    - In a small bowl or ramekin, combine the extra virgin olive oil, fresh lemon juice, and finely grated lemon zest.
    - Whisk lightly with a fork until slightly emulsified.
    - Stir in the sliced fresh basil and red pepper flakes (if using).
1. **Dress & Serve:**
    - Spoon the infused olive oil drizzle evenly across all the tomato and mozzarella slices.
    - Finish with a final pinch of flaky sea salt and freshly cracked black pepper.
    - Serve immediately with crusty bread to sop up the mingled tomato juices and herb oil.

## Pro-Tips & Variations

- **The Tomato Juice Trick:** Do not skip pre-salting the tomatoes. The natural tomato water mingles with the lemon-infused olive oil at the bottom of the plate to create an effortless dressing.
- **Carpaccio Style Variation:** For a punchier izakaya/bistro finish (similar to a seafood carpaccio dressing), whisk in 1/2 tsp of soy sauce or 1/2 tsp of Dijon mustard to deepen the savory umami notes.
