300 grams coleslaw mix with cabbage and carrots\*


   86 g mayonnaise

   19 g white sugar

   16 g lemon juice

   11 g tablespoon white vinegar

    a pinch teaspoon kosher salt

   1 g coarse ground black pepper


    Add the mayonnaise, sugar, lemon juice, white vinegar, salt and pepper to a large mixing bowl and whisk together until fully combined.

    Add in the coleslaw mix and stir well to combine.

    Refrigerate for at least 2 hours before serving.
