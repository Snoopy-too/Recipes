# Tataki Kyūri (Smashed Cucumber Salad)

Crisp side dish for 4 servings (400 g cucumbers)

## Base Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Japanese cucumbers** *(kyūri)*, trimmed | 400 g |
| **Soy sauce** | 15 g |
| **Rice vinegar** *(komezu)* | 15 g |
| **Toasted sesame oil** | 8 g |
| **Sugar** | 3 g |
| **Toasted white sesame seeds** *(iri-goma)* | 3 g |

## Optional Boosters *(to taste)*

| Accent | Amount |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Grated garlic** *(fresh or paste)* | 2 g |
| **Chicken bouillon powder** | 1.5–2 g |
| **Rāyu** *(chili oil)* | 2–3 g |

## Instructions

### 1. Smash & Cut

Place the cucumbers on a cutting board. Lightly whack them along their length with the flat of a knife or a rolling pin until they split open. Break or cut into bite-sized chunks (2–3 cm) so the rough edges catch the dressing.

### 2. Mix Dressing

Whisk the **soy sauce**, **rice vinegar**, **sugar**, and **sesame oil** (plus any optional garlic or chicken bouillon) in a bowl until the sugar fully dissolves.

### 3. Toss & Chill

About **15–20 minutes** before serving, toss the smashed cucumbers with the dressing and toasted sesame seeds. Keep chilled in the fridge until serving to ensure maximum crispness.

## Chef's Tips & Notes

- **Smashing vs. Slicing:** Smashing breaks cucumber cells irregularly, giving rough edges and micro-crannies where the dressing instantly adheres without diluting.
- **Timing:** Dress and toss 15–20 minutes before serving. Prolonged marination draws out too much moisture via osmosis, turning the cucumbers limp.
