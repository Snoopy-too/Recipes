#  Hard Rock Cafe Copycat Tupelo Chicken Tenders Recipe (1 kg Scale)

This recipe recreates the classic spicy cornflake-crusted Tupelo Chicken Tenders from Hard Rock Cafe, fully scaled for 1 kilogram of chicken tenders and converted to metric gram measurements for precise prep. It includes both signature dipping sauces and is tailored for frying at 180°C.

## Overview & Specifications

| Yield | Prep Time | Fry Temp | Cook Time Per Batch | Internal Temp |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4–6 Servings (approx. 20–24 tenders) | 20 minutes | 180°C (356°F) | 3–4 minutes | 74°C (165°F) |

## Ingredients (Measured in Grams)

### 1. Chicken & Breading

| Ingredient | Quantity (g) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chicken Breast Tenders | 1,000 g | Patted dry with paper towels; tendons trimmed |
| All-Purpose Flour | 125 g | For preliminary double-dredging |
| Corn Flake Crumbs | 100 g | Finely crushed (not powder) for optimal crunch |
| Crushed Red Pepper Flakes | 6 g | Mixed into corn flake crumbs |
| Fine Salt | 6 g | Mixed into corn flake crumbs |
| Cayenne Pepper | 3 g | Mixed into corn flake crumbs |
| Ground Cumin | 3 g | Mixed into corn flake crumbs |
| Paprika | 1.5 g | Mixed into corn flake crumbs |
| Onion Powder | 1 g | Mixed into corn flake crumbs |
| Garlic Powder | 1 g | Mixed into corn flake crumbs |
| Neutral Frying Oil | As needed | Canola, peanut, or vegetable oil (fill pot \~5-8 cm deep) |

### 2. Wet Egg Wash

| Ingredient | Quantity (g) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Whole Milk | 240 g | Whisked with eggs |
| Eggs | 100 g | Approx. 2 large eggs |

### 3. Signature Dipping Sauces

| Sauce | Ingredient | Quantity (g) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Honey Mustard Sauce** | Mayonnaise | 120 g |
| **Honey Mustard Sauce** | Honey | 14 g |
| **Honey Mustard Sauce** | Prepared Yellow Mustard | 10 g |
| **Honey Mustard Sauce** | Paprika | 0.5 g |
| **Classic Apricot Sauce** | Dijon Mustard (Grey Poupon) | 30 g |

## Step-by-Step Instructions

- **Prepare Sauces:** Whisk together the Honey Mustard ingredients in one bowl until smooth. In a second bowl, whisk together the Apricot Sauce ingredients until completely combined. Cover both bowls and refrigerate to allow flavors to meld.
- **Heat the Oil:** Fill a deep fryer or heavy pot with neutral oil (at least 5–8 cm deep) and heat to **180°C (356°F)**. Use a digital thermometer to monitor temperature stability.
- **Set Up Dredging Station:** Arrange three wide, shallow bowls in sequence:
    - *Bowl 1:* 125 g all-purpose flour.
    - *Bowl 2:* 100 g beaten eggs and 240 g milk whisked together.
    - *Bowl 3:* 100 g corn flake crumbs mixed thoroughly with the red pepper flakes, salt, cayenne, cumin, paprika, onion powder, and garlic powder.
- **Double-Dredge the Chicken:** Working with 4–5 tenders at a time:
    - Coat lightly in flour (Bowl 1) and shake off excess.
    - Dip into egg/milk wash (Bowl 2).
    - Dredge back in flour (Bowl 1) for a second light coating.
    - Dip back into egg/milk wash (Bowl 2).
    - Firmly press into the seasoned corn flake mixture (Bowl 3) until fully and evenly coated. Place on a wire rack or parchment paper while finishing the remaining tenders.
- **Deep Fry at 180°C:** Carefully lower 4–5 tenders into the hot oil. Fry for **3 to 4 minutes**, turning occasionally, until the crust is deep golden brown and crisp. Check with an instant-read meat thermometer to ensure the internal temperature reaches **74°C (165°F)**.
- **Drain and Serve:** Remove tenders using tongs or a spider skimmer and transfer to a wire cooling rack set over a baking sheet (or paper towels). Repeat for remaining batches. Serve immediately with the chilled sauces.

## Chef's Notes & Best Practices

- **Oil Temperature Management:** Adding cold chicken will cause oil temperature to drop. Do not crowd the pot—keep batches to 4–5 tenders and allow the oil to recover back to 180°C before adding the next batch.
- **Cornflake Texture:** Crush cornflakes by placing them in a sealed zip-top bag and rolling with a rolling pin until coarse crumb size is reached. Do not pulse in a food processor into a fine dust, as the coarse crunch is essential to the signature Hard Rock texture.
- **Holding Warm:** If serving a large group at once, keep fried tenders on a wire rack in an oven preheated to 95°C (200°F) while frying the subsequent batches.
