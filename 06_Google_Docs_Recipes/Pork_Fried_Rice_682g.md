Pork Fried Rice (Scaled for 682g Rice)

**Scaling:** 1.36x factor (from 500g base to 682g cooked rice / 310g raw rice = 2 house cups).

# Ingredients (Scaled for 682g Cooked Rice)

- **Cooked white rice (COLD!):** 682 g (310 g raw rice = 2 house cups)
- **Cooked pork:** 205 g (diced)
- **Eggs:** 3 large eggs (beaten)
- **Mixed frozen vegetables:** 205 g (approx. 1 1/3 cups peas, carrots, etc.)
- **Garlic:** 12 g (minced)
- **Fresh ginger:** 14 g (minced)
- **Green onions:** 55 g (whites and dark greens separated)
- **Cooking oil (total):** 61 g (divided into cooking steps)
- **Soy sauce:** 41 g
- **Oyster sauce:** 14 g
- **Sesame oil:** 7 g
- **White pepper:** 1.4 g (or generous pinch)
- **Sugar:** 2.7 g (approx. 1/2 tsp)
- **Shaoxing wine:** 3–4 g (approx. 3/4 tsp)
- **Salt:** To taste
- **Red pepper flakes:** To taste

## Chef Adjustment Options

**Lighter Oil Option**Reduce total cooking oil to **41 g**. Add a splash of **14–20 g** (approx. 1 tbsp) of water during the rice-frying step to prevent sticking.


Stronger Pork PresenceIncrease cooked pork to 273 g.


Japanese Soy Sauce ControlIf using sharper/saltier Japanese soy sauce, use **34 g soy sauce + 7 g water**.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

**1. Prep Everything**Have all ingredients chopped, measured, and placed within reach by the stove. Fried rice cooks very fast!


**2. Scramble the Eggs**


- Heat 1 tbsp of cooking oil in a wok or large skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.
- Pour in beaten eggs. Let set slightly, then scramble gently into soft curds.
- Immediately remove to a clean plate and set aside.


**3. Cook Aromatics & Vegetables**


- Add another tbsp of oil to the wok; increase heat to high.
- Add minced garlic, minced ginger, and the white/light green parts of the green onions. Stir-fry 15–30 seconds until fragrant.
- Add mixed vegetables. Stir-fry 2–3 minutes until crisp-tender. *(If using raw ground pork instead, cook and brown it here, draining excess fat).*


**4. Cook the Pork**


- Push vegetables to one side of the wok.
- Add remaining oil to the cleared space and add the diced cooked pork.
- Stir-fry 1–2 minutes until heated through and lightly crisped at the edges.
- Toss pork together with the vegetables.


**5. Add the Rice**


- Add the cold rice.
- Break up any clumps with your spatula.
- Stir-fry constantly for 2–3 minutes, pressing rice against the hot wok surface to dry it out and develop toasted wok flavor.


**6. Combine & Season**


- Return scrambled eggs to wok, breaking into smaller pieces.
- Pour soy sauce and oyster sauce evenly over the rice.
- Add white pepper, sugar, and Shaoxing wine (plus red pepper flakes if using).
- Stir-fry vigorously for 1–2 minutes until everything is evenly seasoned and piping hot. Taste and adjust salt/pepper/soy sauce if needed.


**7. Finish & Serve**


- Remove wok from heat.
- Drizzle sesame oil over top and scatter sliced dark green scallion tops.
- Toss one final time and serve immediately!
