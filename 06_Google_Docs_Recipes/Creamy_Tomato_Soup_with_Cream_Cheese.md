Creamy Tomato Soup with Cream Cheese

A rich, velvety tomato soup made with canned whole tomato puree, simmered with aromatics and finished with tempered cream cheese for a smooth texture without curdling.

# Ingredients

- **Tomato puree:** 775 ml (pureed canned whole tomatoes)
- **Cream cheese:** 60 to 90 g (about 2 to 3 heaping tbsp), softened at room temperature
- **Chicken broth / stock:** 300 to 400 ml
- **Butter:** 2 tbsp (or olive oil)
- **Yellow onion:** 1 medium, finely diced
- **Garlic:** 3 cloves, minced
- **Tomato paste:** 1 tbsp (optional, for deeper flavor)
- **Herbs:** 1 bay leaf, a pinch of dried oregano or Italian seasoning, plus fresh basil (optional)
- **Sugar or honey:** ½ tsp (optional, to balance acidity if needed)
- **Salt and freshly cracked black pepper:** to taste

## Instructions

1. **Sweat the Aromatics:** Melt 2 tablespoons of butter in a pot over medium-low heat. Add the diced onion and a small pinch of salt. Cook gently for 8 to 10 minutes until completely soft and translucent without browning.
1. **Add Garlic & Tomato Paste:** Stir in the minced garlic, dried herbs, and tomato paste. Cook for 60 to 90 seconds until fragrant and the paste turns a deeper brick-red color.
1. **Simmer the Soup:** Pour in the 775 ml of tomato puree and 300 ml of chicken broth. Drop in the bay leaf. Bring to a low simmer, cover partially, and cook for 15 to 20 minutes to meld flavors and eliminate raw acidity.
1. **Blend the Base:** Turn off the heat and discard the bay leaf. Use an immersion blender directly in the pot until the onions and broth are completely smooth.
1. **Temper & Melt Cream Cheese:** Ladle about ½ cup (120 ml) of the warm soup into a small bowl containing the softened cream cheese. Whisk or mash with a fork until smooth and uniform. Stir this mixture back into the main pot over low heat until fully incorporated and creamy.
1. **Season & Adjust:** Taste for balance. If the tomatoes taste too sharp, stir in ½ teaspoon of sugar or honey. If too thick, thin with the remaining chicken broth. Finish with salt, black pepper, and fresh torn basil before serving.
