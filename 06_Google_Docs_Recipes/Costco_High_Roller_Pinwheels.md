### **Prep & Yield**

- **Prep Time:** 15 minutes
- **Chill Time:** 30–60 minutes *(essential for clean slices)*
- **Yield:** \~20–24 pinwheel rolls

### **Ingredients**

#### **The Creamy Herb Spread ("Glue")**

- **200g** cream cheese, softened
- **60g** mayonnaise or sour cream
- **1 packet (1 oz)** Ranch seasoning mix (or 1 tbsp dried dill, garlic powder, onion powder, and grated Parmesan)
- **1 tbsp** fresh chives or green onions, finely chopped

#### **The Fillings**

- **4 large** lavash flatbreads (or 10-inch flour tortillas)
- **½ lb** deli turkey breast, thinly sliced
- **8–10 strips** thick-cut bacon, cooked crisp and chopped (or bacon bits)
- **8 slices** Swiss cheese or Provolone
- **2 cups** fresh spinach leaves or romaine/butter lettuce, washed and thoroughly dried
- **2 medium** Roma tomatoes, seeded and finely diced *(seeding is critical so the wrap doesn't get soggy)*

### **Step-by-Step Instructions**

**1.Mix the Spread:**Blends flavors and acts as the structural glue.

In a medium bowl, combine the softened cream cheese, mayonnaise, Ranch seasoning, and chives. Beat or stir until completely smooth and spreadable.

**2.Layer the Flatbreads:**Spread all the way to the edges.

Lay flatbreads on a clean work surface. Evenly spread a generous layer of the cream cheese mixture over each flatbread, making sure to cover all the way to the edges (this seals the roll).

**3.Add Cheese, Meats, and Veggies:**

Layer the ingredients evenly over the cream cheese:

- Swiss/Provolone cheese slices
- Deli turkey breast
- Crispy bacon pieces
- Dried tomatoes and fresh greens

**4.Roll Tightly:**

Starting from one side, roll the flatbread up as tightly as possible without squeezing out the filling.

**5.Chill and Slice:**Don't skip chilling.

Wrap each rolled log tightly in plastic wrap and refrigerate for **at least 30 to 60 minutes**. Once set, use a sharp serrated knife to trim the outer ends, then slice each log into **¾-inch to 1-inch** pinwheels.

### **Pro Tips for Costco-Quality Results**

- **Deseed the tomatoes:** Scoop out the watery core and seeds before dicing. Excess moisture is the \#1 reason roll-ups fall apart.
- **Use Lavash bread:** Costco uses soft lavash flatbread, which rolls without cracking better than traditional flour tortillas.
- **Make-Ahead Friendly:** You can make the wrapped logs up to 24 hours in advance—just slice right before serving!
