    

    1-1/2 teaspoons garlic salt = 7.5 grams
    1-1/2 teaspoons onion powder = 5.5 grams
    1-1/2 teaspoons white sugar = 7.5 grams
    1 tablespoon dried oregano = 6 grams
    1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper = 1.5 grams
    1/8 teaspoon dried thyme = 0.3 grams
    1/2 teaspoon dried basil = 0.5 grams
    1-1/2 teaspoons dried parsley = 1.5 grams
    1/8 teaspoon celery salt = 0.6 grams
    1 tablespoon salt = 18 grams

Directions
Instructions Checklist

    Step 1

    In a small bowl, mix together the garlic salt, 
    onion powder, sugar, oregano, pepper, thyme, 
    basil, parsley, celery salt and regular salt. 
    Store in a tightly sealed container.
    
    Step 2

    To prepare dressing, whisk together 1/4 cup white vinegar, 
    2/3 cup canola oil, 2 tablespoons water and 
    2 tablespoons of the dry mix.
