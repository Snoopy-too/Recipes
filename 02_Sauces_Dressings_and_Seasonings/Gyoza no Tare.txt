Kansai-Style Savory Gyoza Tare (in grams)
Ingredients:

30g soy sauce (light or regular Japanese shoyu)

15g rice vinegar (unseasoned)

10g liquid dashi stock (light kombu or katsuo dashi) 1g powder + 9g water

5g mirin

2g sesame oil

2g sugar

(Optional) finely grated ginger or a drop of fresh lemon juice for freshness
