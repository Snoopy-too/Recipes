Carne Asada Seasoning Recipe

Ingredients (Doubled & in Grams):

  • 2 tablespoons chili powder (12 g)
  • 2 tablespoons garlic powder (16 g)
  • 2 teaspoons onion powder (6 g)
  • 2 teaspoons Mexican oregano (2 g)
  • 2 teaspoons salt (12 g)
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin (2.3 g)
  • 1 teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional for heat) (1 g)
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper (2.3 g)
  • Optional: 2 teaspoons crystallized lime powder or dried lime zest (4 g)
