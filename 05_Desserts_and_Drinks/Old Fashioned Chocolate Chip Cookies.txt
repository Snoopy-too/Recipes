Old Fashioned Chocolate Chip Cookies

Butter: ¾ cup →                170 grams
Granulated sugar: ½ cup →       75 grams
Light brown sugar: 1 cup →     150 grams
Large eggs:  →                   2 (50 grams each)
Vanilla extract: 1 tablespoon → 13 grams
Baking soda: 1 teaspoon →        5 grams
Salt: ½ teaspoon →             2.5 grams
All-purpose flour: 2 cups →    240 grams
Chocolate chips: 2 cups →      340 grams



Half Recipe:

Old Fashioned Chocolate Chip Cookies

Butter: ¾ cup →                 85 grams
Granulated sugar:  →            38 grams
Light brown sugar:  →           75 grams
Large eggs:  →                   1 (50 grams)
Vanilla extract:  →              7 grams
Baking soda:  →                  3 grams
Salt: ½ teaspoon →             1.5 grams
All-purpose flour:  →          120 grams
Chocolate chips: →             170 grams


1. Mix butter sugar and brown sugar in a bowl until smooth.
2. Add eggs and vanilla extract and mix completely.
3. Add baking soda and salt.
4. Slowly add flour until completely mixed.
5. fold in chocolate chips.
6. Drop full spoons onto the oven pan's baking sheet.
7. Bake at 190°C for about 12 minutes.
