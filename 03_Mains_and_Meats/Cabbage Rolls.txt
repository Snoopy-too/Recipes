---Ingredients
---For the Cabbage Rolls:

1 large head of green cabbage
1 lb ground beef (or a mix of beef and pork)
1/2 cup cooked rice (cooled)
1 small onion, finely chopped
2 cloves garlic, minced
1 egg
1 tsp salt
1/2 tsp black pepper
1/2 tsp smoked paprika
1/2 tsp dried oregano


---For the Tomato Sauce:

1 tbsp olive oil
1 small onion, finely chopped
2 cloves garlic, minced
1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes
1 tbsp tomato paste
1 cup beef broth
1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
1 tsp sugar (optional, to balance acidity)
1/2 tsp salt
1/2 tsp black pepper
1/2 tsp dried basil


---Adjusted Cooking Plan:

Preheat oven to 300°F (150°C).
Cooking time: 3 hours (instead of 1.5 hours at 350°F).
Keep the pot tightly covered to retain moisture.
If the sauce thickens too much, you can add a little extra beef broth midway through.
Rest for 10 minutes before serving.
