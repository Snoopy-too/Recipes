Stuffed Cabbage Rolls

Ingredients

For the Cabbage Rolls:

  • 1 large head of green cabbage
  • 1 lb ground beef (or a mix of beef and pork)
  • 1/2 cup cooked rice (cooled)
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp dried oregano

For the Tomato Sauce:

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 (400g / 14 oz) can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 cup beef broth
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar (optional)
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp dried basil

Instructions

1. Prepare the Cabbage

  1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil.
  2. Remove the core from the cabbage and place the whole head in the water for 2-3 minutes.
  3. Peel off the softened outer leaves and repeat until you have about 12.
  4. Trim the thick stem from the base of each leaf.

2. Make the Filling

  1. Mix ground meat, rice, onion, garlic, egg, salt, pepper, paprika, and oregano in a bowl.

3. Assemble the Rolls

  1. Place 2 tablespoons of filling in each cabbage leaf.
  2. Fold in sides and roll tightly.

4. Make the Sauce

  1. Heat olive oil in a skillet. Sauté onion and garlic until soft.
  2. Add crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, broth, Worcestershire sauce, sugar, salt, pepper, and basil. Simmer 5-10 minutes.

5. Cook the Rolls

  1. Preheat oven to 300°F (150°C).
  2. Spread a thin layer of sauce in the bottom of a stainless steel pot.
  3. Place rolls seam-side down. Pour remaining sauce on top.
  4. Cover tightly and bake for 3 hours.

6. Serve

  1. Let rest for 10 minutes before serving.
  2. Garnish with parsley or Parmesan if desired.
