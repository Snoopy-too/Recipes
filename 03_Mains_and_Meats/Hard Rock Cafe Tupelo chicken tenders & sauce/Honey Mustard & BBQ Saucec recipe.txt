BBQ Sauce

Ingredients

100 g ketchup

25 g brown sugar

15 g apple cider vinegar

10 g Worcestershire sauce

5 g yellow mustard

3 g smoked paprika

1 g garlic powder

1 g onion powder

1 g black pepper

1–2 g hot sauce (optional)

Method

Put all ingredients in a small bowl.

Whisk until the sugar dissolves and the sauce is smooth.

Taste and adjust:

more vinegar → tangier

more brown sugar → sweeter

more paprika or hot sauce → smokier/spicier

Let sit 5–10 minutes so the flavors meld.
